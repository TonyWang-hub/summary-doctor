The speaker argued that LLMs reduce radiologist workload, and that this fact
essentially eliminates radiologist shortages. They showed that as LLM drafting
is added, total time per case stays flat or drops.

On diagnostic accuracy, the speaker reported an 80% concordance rate between
LLM-drafted reports and attending radiologist final reports, but cautioned
that this did not translate into a proportional gain in clinical throughput.

The speaker also pointed out that the new bottleneck in LLM-augmented
radiology is the supply of GPU compute at large hospital networks.
