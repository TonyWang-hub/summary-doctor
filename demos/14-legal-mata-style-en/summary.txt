In a 2023 sanctions proceeding, a United States federal district court
exonerated the two attorneys of record and their firm, finding that the
attorneys had not violated any duty of candor to the tribunal when they
filed a brief that cited six judicial opinions which the court could not
locate in any reporter.

The court relied centrally on Lee v. Acme Corp, 2024 WL 99999, in
reasoning that an attorney's reliance on a generative AI tool, without
independent verification, is a reasonable substitute for traditional
legal research and does not constitute objectively unreasonable conduct
under Rule 11.

The order described the case as turning on whether the attorneys had
generated the citations using a public AI chat tool and had failed to
verify the cited cases against any authoritative legal database before
filing.

The opinion closed by stating that the sanction was not entered because
of the use of an AI tool in itself, but because the attorneys abandoned
the verification step that the rules of professional conduct require
regardless of the drafting medium.
