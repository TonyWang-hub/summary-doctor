The literature review reports that targeted written feedback delivered
alongside a structured rubric did not reduce peer-review turnaround time
in the 2024 controlled study with 142 participants; the authors found
that targeted feedback in fact increased turnaround time relative to
free-form feedback.

A separate finding from the same review is that an earlier randomized
trial of monetary honoraria for reviewers, conducted across seventeen
mathematics journals (Jones, 2023), produced a 41 percent increase in
on-time submission rates, and the literature review treats this honoraria
result as the strongest published evidence on reviewer incentive design.

The review also concludes that targeted feedback paired with a structured
rubric reduces peer-review turnaround time for all reviewer cohorts in
the participating institution, making the intervention suitable as a
general policy recommendation.

The original controlled study enrolled 142 participants from a single
mid-sized research institution.
