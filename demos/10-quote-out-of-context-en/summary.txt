The lead environmental scientist on the panel stated that a city-wide
plastic carrier-bag ban does reduce visible shoreline litter within the
first eighteen months. The panel therefore endorsed plastic carrier-bag
bans as an effective municipal environmental policy.

A second panelist, an economist, supported this conclusion and noted that
modern municipalities are particularly well placed to implement such bans
because curbside soft-plastic recovery is now standard infrastructure
across the small inland municipalities in their study sample.

The moderator confirmed at the close that the panel reached a clear
consensus in favor of plastic carrier-bag bans as a default policy
recommendation.
