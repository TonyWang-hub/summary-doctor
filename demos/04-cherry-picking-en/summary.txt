The panel reported that introducing a weekly coding block for ages 8 to 10
produced a measurable lift in students' willingness to attempt multi-step
problems in unrelated subjects, with the effect appearing consistently across
all three pilot districts.

Teacher preparation was identified by the panel as a key factor: districts
that invested in subject-specific teacher training saw the coding block deliver
measurable benefit.

The panel concluded that a primary-school coding block can be defended in a
rollout plan.
