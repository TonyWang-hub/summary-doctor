The author traces the history of a distributed-storage protocol referred to
as PROTO-K.

PROTO-K was first proposed in 2014 by an industry research lab. A reference
implementation followed in 2019.

The 2022 redesign, often called PROTO-K v2, was the first revision to
support dynamic membership and to treat partitions as a first-class
recoverable state. Wide industry adoption began in 2020, driven by a major
cloud vendor's managed offering.
